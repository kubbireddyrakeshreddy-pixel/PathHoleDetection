How RoadSafe Works

From photo to government action in just a few steps.

01
📷
Capture & Upload
Take a photo of the damaged road. Your device's GPS automatically tags the location.
02
🤖
AI Detection
YOLOv11 analyses the image in under 2 seconds, detecting potholes in any lighting or weather.
03
📧
Auto Alert
The nearest government officer is instantly alerted by email with the image and GPS coordinates.
04
📊
Track Progress
Follow your report through Reviewed → Work in Progress → Repaired in real time.
